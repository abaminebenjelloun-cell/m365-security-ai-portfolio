# Discovery workshop: Copilot and agents (half day)

**Audience:** IT lead, security or data protection officer, one or two business owners with an AI use case, the sponsor for the first 20 and the last 30 minutes.
**Outcome:** a shared picture of readiness, two or three prioritised use cases, and the scope of the next step.

## Before the workshop (sent one week ahead)

- Licence summary (Microsoft 365 plan, Copilot licences, Power Platform licences, add-ons)
- Read-only admin access or a screen-share slot for the live checks
- List of AI tools people already use, if known
- One page per candidate use case: who, what data, what result

## Agenda

| Time | Block | Content | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Goals (sponsor present) | Why AI now, what success looks like in 6 months, constraints (revDSG, FINMA if relevant, budget) | Success criteria |
| 0:20 | How Copilot sees data | Copilot respects permissions; live look at sharing settings, one overshared site, label status | Shared understanding of the risk |
| 0:50 | Readiness check | Labels, DLP, oversharing, web grounding, shadow AI, agent settings, capacity (checklist from 01 and 02) | Readiness heat map (green, orange, red) |
| 1:40 | Break | | |
| 1:50 | Use cases | Each owner presents; scored on value, data sensitivity, feasibility (M365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Foundry) | Ranked use cases |
| 2:40 | Governance and adoption model | Who can build, who approves, who pays (agent lifecycle, capacity); who will be the AI champions per department | Draft RACI, champion candidates |
| 3:10 | Next steps (sponsor present) | Recommended path: AI readiness phases, first agent, estimate range | Agreed next step and date |

## Scoring grid for use cases

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business value | Nice to have | Saves hours per week for a team | Changes a process or a client service |
| Data sensitivity (inverted) | Secret or personal data at scale | Confidential, limited audience | Internal or public |
| Feasibility | Needs custom models or external data at scale | Copilot Studio with connectors | Microsoft 365 Copilot or a simple agent on SharePoint |
| Readiness of the data | Unlabelled, overshared | Partly cleaned | Owned, labelled, permissions right |

Start with the highest total among cases scoring at least 3 on sensitivity.
