# Proposal outline: AI readiness

A proposal the sponsor can read in ten minutes and the IT lead can execute from.

1. **Context in the client's words** (from the discovery workshop): goals, constraints, success criteria.
2. **What we found** (three to five facts from the readiness check, with numbers): for example the share of sites open to everyone, labels in use or not, AI apps already in use.
3. **Recommended approach:** diagnostic, correction, protection, then the first agent. Why in this order.
4. **Scope:** workstreams included and excluded, the pilot group, the first use case.
5. **Deliverables:** for each phase, what the client receives (inventory and delta report, taxonomy, DLP policies in simulation, decision-gate memos, agent with evaluation results, runbooks).
6. **Decision gates:** the decisions the sponsor will take, with the date range.
7. **Plan:** phases on a timeline, including simulation periods.
8. **Effort and price:** from the estimate template, with assumptions and exclusions.
9. **Roles:** RACI, named contacts, required availability of site owners.
10. **Risks and mitigations:** preview features, licence gaps, owner availability, change resistance.
11. **After the project:** optional run service (monthly Secure Score snapshot, inventory delta, cost report, agent reviews).
